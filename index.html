Connected Unstop accounts

Connect an account (once per account)

1
Add the bookmark

Show the bookmarks bar with Ctrl+Shift+B, then drag this button onto it:

⏰ Connect to Reminder
2
Log in to Unstop

Open unstop.com and log in with the account you want to connect.

3
Click the bookmark

Click ⏰ Connect to Reminder. This app opens; name the account and press Connect.

4
Second account?

Log out of Unstop, log in with the other account, and click the bookmark again.

🔒 Only your Unstop login token is stored, never your Unstop password. If you log out of Unstop the connection may stop; you'll get a notification to reconnect.

🔔 When should I remind you?

Before a round closes

Useful for submission deadlines.

🧰 Tools

🔔
Test notification

Check that Windows notifications show up. Look at the bottom-right of your screen.

📱
Export to calendar

Download an .ics file with all upcoming rounds. Import it into Google Calendar to get alerts on your phone.

🔄
Sync with Unstop

Runs automatically every 6 hours. Picks up new registrations and changed dates.
